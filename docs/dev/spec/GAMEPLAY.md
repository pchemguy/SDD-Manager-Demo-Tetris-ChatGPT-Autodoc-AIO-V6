# Gameplay contracts

## Board and lifecycle

G-01: The visible settled board has integer coordinates `x=0..9`, `y=0..19`, with y increasing downwards. Empty and colored occupied cells are distinct; ordinary pieces never use white. The active piece has kind I/J/L/O/S/T/Z, orientation 0/R/2/L and an integer local-frame origin. Movement permits active cells at `y=-2..19`; settled cells exist only at visible rows. Spawn origin is `(3,-1)` using [ROTATION](ROTATION.md) geometry. A spawn colliding with a visible settled cell immediately causes game over. A lock with any cell above row zero causes game over without writing those cells. A penetrator locks only with all four cells inside the visible board.

G-02: Lifecycle is ready, playing, paused or game-over. Start creates an empty board, score/lines zero, level one, empty hold, zero bonuses and a fresh piece source. Restart starts a fresh game and excludes an abandoned run from results; session leaderboard/settings remain. Game-over disables gameplay commands and emits one completion event per game instance. A level/score is readable independently of Canvas.

G-03: Left/Right move one column, Down attempts one row, Up/X rotates clockwise, Z counterclockwise, Space hard-drops, C/Shift holds, B activates penetrator, P/Escape toggles user pause and Enter starts/restarts when ready/game-over. One-shot actions ignore repeated keydown events. Left/Right/Down repeat after 150 ms and every 50 ms thereafter, using application-controlled repeat rather than operating-system rate. Most recently pressed horizontal direction wins while both are held; releasing it reactivates the other after a fresh repeat delay. Clear held-key state on pause, blur and restart. Ignore gameplay keys when an input/select/textarea/button owns focus; native control actions remain usable. Prevent page scrolling only for handled gameplay keys while the play surface owns input.

Pause has composable reasons: explicit user pause, hidden document/window blur and file operation. Any reason freezes logical time and rejects gameplay commands. Returning focus/visibility removes only its automatic reason; a user pause remains. Restart resets user/automatic gameplay pause and clocks only when the document is visible/focused, and is unavailable during a file operation.

## Gravity and locking

G-04: Gravity interval in milliseconds is `max(70, 1000 × 0.8^(level−1))`. Gravity attempts a one-row move after each interval. After a successful lock/clear/spawn, the next piece's gravity clock starts at spawn time. A level change applies to subsequent gravity scheduling; an existing grounding deadline retains its captured interval. A successful downward player move resets the gravity schedule from its command time. Lateral moves and rotations do not reset gravity.

G-05: A legal landing is a fully visible, non-overlapping placement from which the active movement policy cannot progress downward toward a later legal target. For an ordinary piece it is the first obstruction/floor below the current position. For a penetrator it is its deepest valid projected row. Becoming grounded starts an interval-mode deadline of one whole current gravity interval. Movement/rotation that stays grounded preserves that deadline; leaving the ground cancels it; regrounding starts a fresh interval. Snapshot general and hard-drop locking policies at piece spawn, including hold swaps. Pause preserves remaining deadline duration. Processing elapsed time fires due deadlines before same-time player input.

In `failed-down` mode there is no deadline: a failed gravity or Down attempt at a legal landing locks the piece. Failed horizontal/rotation attempts never lock. In `interval` mode failed downward attempts do not shorten the deadline. A hard drop with `immediate` policy locks its legal target in that command; with `follow`, it follows the captured general policy. In failed-down/follow mode, hard drop positions the piece and the next failed downward attempt locks it; hard drop itself is not that attempt. A zero-distance follow-policy hard drop never resets gravity or an existing grounding deadline.

Immediately after successful descent, drop, rotation, horizontal movement or spawn, reevaluate grounding. Reject a transform that violates the applicable bounds/collision/viability predicates. The domain must not produce an invalid settled state if a command is unavailable or rejected.

## Scoring and pieces

G-06: A successful lock writes four cells, removes all full rows simultaneously and compacts rows downward. Award `0/100/300/500/800 × level-before-clear` points for zero/one/two/three/four lines. Set level to `floor(totalLines/10)+1`. Successful soft-drop rows award one point each; hard drop awards two per row moved, including penetrator travel. No T-spin, combo or back-to-back bonus is required. Scores and counters are safe non-negative integers; score additions saturate at `9007199254740991` rather than overflowing. Timestamp creation is outside the game domain.

G-07: Classic delivery may use independent uniform draws. The complete system uses a seven-bag source: every consecutive bag contains each kind exactly once, shuffled by Fisher–Yates using injected uniform randomness. Maintain at least one queued next piece; preview never consumes it. A fresh game creates a fresh source. Disabled next preview hides its panel content without changing the queue.

G-08: Hold is usable once for the current piece until a successful lock. Empty hold stores the current kind and consumes the next queued kind; nonempty hold exchanges kinds without consuming the queue. The replacement spawns in orientation zero with fresh gravity/deadline and current captured locking policies. A blocked replacement spawn causes game over. Rejected hold does not alter source/hold state. Disabling hold preserves the held kind and used flag; re-enabling does not grant an extra swap. Hold is rejected whenever penetrator is active, regardless of the hold setting.

G-09: Ordinary hard drop selects the last non-overlapping row reachable before the first downward collision. Penetrator hard drop uses P-02's deepest target. The ghost uses that same target even when hard drop is disabled. Hidden ghost never affects movement. For ghost mode `show`, render a distinguishable translucent/outlined projection; for `hide`, do not render it; for `dynamic`, render only when every projected cell has `y>=10`. The active piece is drawn over an identical-position ghost. White penetrator ghost styling remains distinguishable from the active white piece. No target means no ghost and no drop; valid active penetrator states must retain a target.

## Penetrator contracts

P-01: Activation is available only while playing, with a normal active piece, at least one bonus and a fully visible legal target at or below the current origin for the current x/orientation. On success spend exactly one bonus and mark the piece active until lock/game-over/restart. Repeated activation, unavailable bonus, pause or no target is a no-op with no spending. No manual deactivation/refund or hold transfer exists. Reevaluate grounding without restarting an already-running deadline when the piece remains grounded.

P-02: While active, ignore occupied cells for candidate movement/rotation traversal; enforce x bounds, `y>=-2`, bottom boundary and existence of a fully visible non-overlapping target at or below the candidate origin. Enumerate candidate origin rows through the bottom limit and choose the greatest valid y, even when intermediate rows overlap occupied cells. Reject moves or rotations with no such target. Gravity/Down moves one row toward the current deepest target, crossing occupied cells without deleting or changing them, and stops at that target. Rotation still uses the ordered SRS candidate sequence, but each candidate uses penetrator viability rather than ordinary occupied-cell collision. A lock independently rechecks full visibility and non-overlap; transient overlap cannot trigger a lock or failed-down lock-out. The active piece and its four cells are entirely white.

P-03: Each successful four-line clear awards one bonus, up to three; excess awards are discarded. This also applies to an activated penetrator's clear. The current activation ends on lock before the award, so a resulting bonus belongs to future activations. Display exactly one lightning symbol per stored bonus and a readable count/label. Activation and awards update the indicator in the same rendered transition. New games start with zero; hold/preview/visibility settings do not create bonuses.
